Large ($150-300, 0.6-1.0 cu ft):

Insider Tips Nobody Tells You

  1. Convection affects everything — A convection toaster oven cooks 25-30°F hotter than the dial setting. If a recipe says 350F, set your convection oven to 325F. If you burn food regularly, this is why.

  2. Position matters more than temperature — The sweet spot in most toaster ovens is the center, 1-2 inches from the bottom. Too high = burned top, raw bottom. Too low = burned bottom, pale top. Adjust the rack, not the oven.

  3. Preheat for everything except frozen food — For room-temperature food, preheat 3-5 minutes. For frozen food, skip preheat (the food acts as a heatsink anyway and the preheat is wasted).

FAQ

Can a toaster oven replace a full-size oven? For most daily cooking: yes. Toaster ovens preheat faster (3-5 min vs 15-20 min), use less energy (1800W vs 4000W), and don’t heat up your kitchen. For large items (whole turkey, 9x13 lasagna, multiple sheet pans), you still need a full oven.

What size toaster oven should I buy? 0.3 cu ft: 1-2 people, fits 9-inch pizza. 0.5 cu ft: family of 3, fits 12-inch pizza. 0.6+ cu ft: family of 4+, fits 9x13 baking pans. Measure your counter depth before buying.

Is the Breville worth 5x the price of a budget toaster oven? If you toast bread 2-3 times per week: no. If you cook full meals (roasted chicken, sheet pan vegetables, frozen pizza, baked fish) in your toaster oven 4-5 times per week: yes. The Breville’s even cooking, accurate temperature, and air fry function genuinely replace 3 appliances.

How do I clean a toaster oven? Empty the crumb tray after every use (seriously — it’s a fire hazard if it fills). Wipe interior with a damp cloth monthly. For stuck-on food, bake at 400F for 10 minutes to loosen residue, then wipe. Never use oven cleaner on non-stick interiors.

Why does my toaster oven burn one side of food? Uneven heating from exposed elements. Most ovens under $100 have tubular elements that create hot spots. Position food in the center, rotate the pan halfway through cooking, and avoid overcrowding. This is one area where the Breville’s Element IQ genuinely solves a problem.
