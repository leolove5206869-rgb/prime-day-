Best Electric Kettle for Tea — Temperature Control Brewing Guide

3 min read

Brewing tea at the right temperature makes a bigger difference than the tea itself. Green tea boiled at 212°F turns bitter. The same leaves at 175°F taste sweet and vegetal. An electric kettle with temperature control is the single best upgrade a tea drinker can make.

For our full kettle comparison, read the best electric kettle guide.

What to Look For in a Tea Kettle

Temperature presets are the most important feature. The best tea kettles offer presets at 160°F (delicate white), 175°F (green), 185°F (oolong), and 200°F (black). Manual dials work too, but presets make daily brewing effortless.

Keep-warm function lets you boil once and brew multiple cups over an hour. Without it, you reboil every time, which wastes energy and time.

Capacity matters more for tea than coffee — tea drinkers tend to consume more volume. 1.5L+ is ideal for households.

Spout type is less critical for tea than coffee. Standard spouts are fine for filling a mug or teapot. Gooseneck helps if you pour directly into small gaiwans or strainers.

Temperature Guide by Tea Type

Tea Type Temperature Steep Time
White tea (delicate) 160-170°F 2-3 min
Green tea (Japanese) 170-180°F 1-2 min
Green tea (Chinese) 175-185°F 2-3 min
Oolong (light) 185-195°F 3-4 min
Oolong (dark) 195-205°F 3-5 min
Black tea 200-212°F 3-5 min
Herbal / tisane 212°F (boiling) 5-7 min

These are starting points. Adjust based on your specific tea and taste preferences.
