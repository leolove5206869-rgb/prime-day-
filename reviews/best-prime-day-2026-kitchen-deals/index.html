Prime Day 2026 Kitchen Deals FAQ

When is Prime Day 2026?

Amazon Prime Day 2026 runs Tuesday June 23 through Friday June 26, 2026. This is a four-day event, expanded from the original two-day format.

Are air fryers cheaper on Prime Day?

Yes. Air fryers are the most competitively priced Prime Day kitchen category. Basket-style models from Cosori, Ninja, and Instant Pot typically reach 30–50% below their regular prices during this event.

What kitchen items have the biggest Prime Day discounts?

Based on historical data from 2024 and 2025 Prime Day events, air fryers (30–50% off) and stand mixers (25–40% off) offer the deepest percentage discounts. Espresso machines and high-end blenders offer the largest absolute dollar savings ($150–200 off).

Do Prime Day kitchen deals sell out?

Kitchen Lightning Deals in popular categories (air fryers, espresso machines) frequently sell out within 2–4 hours of dropping. Adding items to your cart before Prime Day starts is the most reliable strategy.

Are Prime Day prices actually lower than Black Friday?

For kitchen appliances specifically, Prime Day prices are typically equal to or slightly lower than Black Friday prices. The main difference is selection — Prime Day has more kitchen inventory, while Black Friday has more electronics and home theater inventory.

Can I stack coupons with Prime Day prices?

Yes. Kitchen brands frequently add clickable coupons (typically 10–15% off) that stack on top of the Prime Day discount. Always look for a coupon checkbox on Amazon product pages during the event.
