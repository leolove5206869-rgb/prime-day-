Best Waffle Maker in 2026 — 5 Models Tested Side-by-Side

June 9, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick Verdict

A waffle maker is a simple machine — heat plates and a timer. But the difference between a $20 and $200 model isn’t just materials: it’s consistency, browning control, and whether every waffle comes out the same.

  • Best overall: Breville Smart Waffle Pro — consistent browning, removable plates, five presets that actually work
  • Best classic round: Cuisinart WMR-CA — simple, reliable, makes perfect round waffles for $35
  • Best premium: All-Clad 99011 — overbuilt, 6 browning levels, heats evenly across the entire plate
  • Best personal: Dash Mini — $10, makes 4-inch waffles, perfect for kids and chaffles
  • Best budget: Hamilton Beach 26031 — flip mechanism, removable plates, $30

Who this is for: Anyone who eats waffles more than once a month. A good waffle maker turns a weekend treat into something you’d actually have on a Tuesday.

What we liked: The $100 Breville makes waffles as good as a diner. The $10 Dash Mini is absurdly useful for its size and price.

What we didn’t: Most waffle makers under $50 have uneven heating. You’ll see dark and light spots on the same waffle.

Belgian vs Classic: How to Choose

Belgian style (Breville, All-Clad, Dash Mini):

  • Deeper pockets — holds more butter and syrup
  • Thicker, fluffier waffles
  • Takes longer to cook (4-6 minutes vs 3-4)
  • Generally more expensive

Classic round (Cuisinart WMR-CA):

  • Thin, crispy waffles that cook faster
  • Shallower grid — less syrup retention but less mess
  • Cheaper than Belgian models
  • More compact storage

Our take: Get Belgian if you want diner-style waffles. Get classic round if you prefer crispy, thin waffles or have limited storage.

How We Tested

Five waffle makers, 30 days, standardized cooking tests. Every model made the same recipes:

  • Buttermilk waffles from scratch (35%) — Golden brown, crispy exterior, fluffy interior, evenness
  • Frozen waffles (20%) — Even reheating, no burning
  • Browning consistency (20%) — Visual inspection across the entire surface, repeatability across batches
  • Convenience (25%) — Preheat time, cleanup, UI quality, storage footprint

The 5 We’d Recommend

The Breville Smart Waffle Pro makes consistently excellent waffles thanks to its “Waffle IQ” system that senses the batter volume and adjusts cook time automatically.

The good: The five browning levels actually produce distinct results — level 2 gives a pale, soft waffle; level 5 is dark and crispy. The removable plates make cleanup trivial (dishwasher safe). Preheat takes about 3 minutes. The audible beep when ready and when the waffle is done means you don’t hover. The cord wrap underneath keeps the counter tidy.

The bad: At 12 pounds, it’s heavy. The stainless steel shows fingerprints. The 1-inch depth is fine for Belgian waffles but won’t satisfy those who want ultra-thick Belgian. The Waffle IQ is clever but not perfect — it occasionally undercooks thin batters.

FAQ

What’s the difference between Belgian and regular waffle makers? Belgian waffle makers have deeper grids (about 1 inch) for thicker, fluffier waffles with deeper pockets. Regular waffle makers (like the Cuisinart WMR-CA) produce thinner, crisper waffles with shallower grids. Belgian waffles take longer to cook but hold more butter and syrup.

Can I make waffles from a mix in these? Yes — all tested models work with any waffle mix. The Breville’s Waffle IQ adjusts cook time automatically for different batters. For box mixes, start with setting 3 or browning level 3 and adjust based on preference.

How do I clean a waffle maker? Let it cool completely, then wipe with a damp cloth. For stuck-on batter, use a soft brush. Never use steel wool or abrasive cleaners. Models with removable plates (Breville, All-Clad, Hamilton Beach) make cleanup significantly easier. For non-removable plate models, a silicone brush works well.

Are nonstick waffle makers safe? All models listed use PFAS-free nonstick coatings. The Hamilton Beach and All-Clad specifically advertise PFAS-free coatings. Older waffle makers (pre-2015) may contain PFOA, but current production is PFOA-free.

Can I make chaffles in a regular waffle maker? Yes — the Dash Mini is the most popular chaffle maker for keto diets, but any waffle maker works. The Dash Mini makes single-serving chaffles that fit a standard slice of bread. The Breville and Cuisinart make larger chaffles.

What’s the best waffle maker for crispy waffles? For the crispiest results, use a classic round waffle maker like the Cuisinart WMR-CA at browning level 4-5, or the Breville at level 5.
