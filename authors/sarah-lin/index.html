Sarah Lin

Product testing specialist, 8 years reviewing kitchen gear

Sarah tests kitchen gadgets, food storage, tools, and small appliances. She specialises in finding the product that delivers 90% of the performance at half the price.

Sarah Lin has been testing and reviewing kitchen products for 8 years. Her background is in consumer product testing, where she learned to separate marketing claims from real-world performance.

She focuses on the categories where the difference between a $20 tool and a $80 tool actually matters — and where it doesn’t.

Sarah’s reviews cover:

  • Kitchen gadgets: Peelers, choppers, mandolines, garlic presses — the small tools you use every day
  • Food storage: Containers, wraps, organization systems — tested for real leak-proof performance
  • Small appliances: Blenders, food processors, coffee gear — performance per dollar is her specialty
  • Coffee: From pour-over to espresso — she tests the full chain from grinder to cup

Testing philosophy: “The best product isn’t always the most expensive one. I test at multiple price points and tell you exactly where the diminishing returns kick in.”
