## Quick Win Recommendations (5 highest-impact changes)

### 1. Convert "Standards We Follow" into an authoritative section (lift: +30 pts)

**Current (48):**
> - **FTC Endorsement Guidelines** — All affiliate relationships are disclosed
> - **Google EEAT Framework** — Our content reflects real Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness
> - **Awin Publisher Code of Conduct** — Affiliate links are clearly labeled

**Suggested rewrite:**
> HomeKitchen Picks follows three content and compliance standards that govern how we test, write, and disclose:
> 
> **FTC Endorsement Guidelines (16 CFR Part 255):** We clearly label every affiliate link and disclose our commercial relationships. If you buy through our links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This funding never affects our scores, coverage decisions, or recommendations.
> 
> **Google EEAT Framework:** The product reviews on this site are the result of real, hands-on testing by named reviewers with specific experience in their categories. Every review shows the author's name, credentials, and exact testing methodology. We follow Google's product review best-practices: testing criteria, performance benchmarks, comparison against alternatives, and honest disclosure of flaws.
> 
> **Awin Publisher Code of Conduct:** As Awin network members, we maintain transparent advertising relationships and clearly distinguish editorial content from sponsored content.

### 2. Expand "Who Tests the Products" with specific data (lift: +25 pts)

**Current (59):**
> Every review is conducted by a specific member of our review team with relevant experience in that product category.

**Suggested rewrite:**
> Every review on HomeKitchen Picks is conducted by a specific member of our review team. Each reviewer has direct category experience:
> 
> - **Mark Chen** — Culinary Arts graduate, 15+ years cooking experience. Tests cookware, knives, and cooking appliances. Each product tested for a minimum of 30 days in his home kitchen.
> - **Sarah Lin** — 8 years of consumer product testing. Specializes in kitchen gadgets, food storage, and small appliances. Tests budget vs premium products side-by-side.
> - **James Wilson** — Mechanical engineering background, 10 years testing consumer tech. Tests precision instruments against certified lab reference standards.
> 
> All reviewers buy their own products at full retail price. No free review units. No sponsored content.

### 3. Convert "First Impressions" → "Specs + First Use" with data (lift: +35 pts)

> | Spec | Detail |

> The fold-out probe mechanism feels precise — unfold activates the screen, fold triggers auto-off. The silicone boot adds drop protection and grip. The auto-rotating 4-way display is readable from any angle.
