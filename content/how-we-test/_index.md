## Our Testing Promise

Every product on HomeKitchen Picks is purchased with our own money. We never accept free products, sponsored reviews, or paid placements. Our only obligation is to you, the reader.

## How We Test

### 1. We Buy Everything

We purchase every product at retail price, just like you would. This means we see the same packaging, the same quality control, and the same out-of-box experience that you will.

### 2. We Test for 30+ Days

Short reviews miss real problems. We use each product for a minimum of 30 days before writing about it. This catches issues that only show up with extended use — wear patterns, performance degradation, reliability problems.

### 3. We Test Side-by-Side

When reviewing multiple products in a category, we test them under identical conditions. Same kitchen, same recipes, same lighting, same measurements. This eliminates variables and gives you true comparisons.

### 4. We Measure What Matters

We don't just describe how products feel — we measure:

- **Temperature accuracy**: Digital thermometers, infrared guns, and thermocouples
- **Weight and dimensions**: Actual measurements, not spec sheet numbers
- **Performance metrics**: Brew times, heat retention, cutting force, noise levels
- **Durability observations**: How the product holds up after 30-90 days of regular use

### 5. We Disclose Everything

Every review includes:

- When we purchased the product
- How long we tested it
- What we measured and how
- What we liked and what we didn't
- Whether we'd buy it again with our own money
