## Our Testing Approach

- **Buy it**: We pay full retail price — no review units, no freebies
- **Test it**: Minimum 30 days of real-world use in a home kitchen
- **Compare it**: Side-by-side against direct competitors
- **Score it**: Build quality, performance, value, ease of use, durability
- **Report it**: Honest findings — if a product fails, we say so
