# About

Welcome to HomeKitchen Picks.

We review home and kitchen products that actually earn a spot in your kitchen. No sponsored fluff, no "everything is amazing" — just honest, hands-on testing by people who cook and live at home.

Every product we cover is **purchased with our own money**, tested in our own kitchen for a minimum of 30 days, and compared against real alternatives. We tell you what's worth your money, what isn't, and why.

## Our Testing Philosophy

We follow a consistent, documented methodology for every review:

1. **Purchase**: We buy every product at full retail price. We do not accept free samples or review units.
2. **Test**: Each product is used daily for at least 30 days in real cooking conditions.
3. **Compare**: Products are tested side-by-side against direct competitors using standardized criteria.
4. **Score**: We score on 5 dimensions — build quality, performance, value, ease of use, and durability.
5. **Review**: Findings are written honestly. If a product fails, we say so.

## Who We Are

We're a small independent team of home cooks and kitchen enthusiasts based in the United States. Our reviewers have backgrounds in culinary arts, product testing, and consumer journalism.

## How We're Funded

This site is funded entirely through affiliate commissions (primarily Amazon Associates). When you buy something through our links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. **This never affects our recommendations** — if a product is bad, we'll tell you. We'd rather lose a commission than lose your trust.

## Editorial Standards

- All reviews are based on first-hand testing, not spec sheets
- We disclose our testing methodology in every review
- Comparison data is collected under identical conditions
- Reviews are updated when products change or new alternatives emerge
- We correct errors promptly and publicly
