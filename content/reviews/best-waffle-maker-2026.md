## Belgian vs Classic: How to Choose

**Belgian style** (Breville, All-Clad, Dash Mini):
- Deeper pockets — holds more butter and syrup
- Thicker, fluffier waffles
- Takes longer to cook (4-6 minutes vs 3-4)
- Generally more expensive

**Classic round** (Cuisinart WMR-CA):
- Thin, crispy waffles that cook faster
- Shallower grid — less syrup retention but less mess
- Cheaper than Belgian models
- More compact storage

**Our take:** Get Belgian if you want diner-style waffles. Get classic round if you prefer crispy, thin waffles or have limited storage.

## FAQ

<div class="faq-section">

**What's the difference between Belgian and regular waffle makers?**
Belgian waffle makers have deeper grids (about 1 inch) for thicker, fluffier waffles with deeper pockets. Regular waffle makers (like the Cuisinart WMR-CA) produce thinner, crisper waffles with shallower grids. Belgian waffles take longer to cook but hold more butter and syrup.

**Can I make waffles from a mix in these?**
Yes — all tested models work with any waffle mix. The Breville's Waffle IQ adjusts cook time automatically for different batters. For box mixes, start with setting 3 or browning level 3 and adjust based on preference.

**How do I clean a waffle maker?**
Let it cool completely, then wipe with a damp cloth. For stuck-on batter, use a soft brush. Never use steel wool or abrasive cleaners. Models with removable plates (Breville, All-Clad, Hamilton Beach) make cleanup significantly easier. For non-removable plate models, a silicone brush works well.

**Are nonstick waffle makers safe?**
All models listed use PFAS-free nonstick coatings. The Hamilton Beach and All-Clad specifically advertise PFAS-free coatings. Older waffle makers (pre-2015) may contain PFOA, but current production is PFOA-free.

**Can I make chaffles in a regular waffle maker?**
Yes — the Dash Mini is the most popular chaffle maker for keto diets, but any waffle maker works. The Dash Mini makes single-serving chaffles that fit a standard slice of bread. The Breville and Cuisinart make larger chaffles.

**What's the best waffle maker for crispy waffles?**
For the crispiest results, use a classic round waffle maker like the Cuisinart WMR-CA at browning level 4-5, or the Breville at level 5.
