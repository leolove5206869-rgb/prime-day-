## 📋 Prime Day Shopping Tips

**1. Add to cart NOW, buy on Day 1**
Amazon lets you add items to your cart before Prime Day starts. Do this. The best deals sell out in the first 6 hours.

**2. Check Lightning Deals at midnight PST**
Prime Day "Lightning Deals" drop at 12:00 AM Pacific on June 23. These are limited-quantity, time-bound discounts that sell out fast. Kitchen Lightning Deals are typically the deepest discounts of the event.

**3. Subscribe & Save stacks with Prime Day prices**
If a product you use regularly (coffee beans, dishwasher detergent, paper towels) has a Prime Day price, set it up as a Subscribe & Save order. You get the Prime Day price + 5-15% S&S discount.

**4. Don't buy the cheapest version of anything**
Manufacturers make "Prime Day specials" with cheaper components. Stick to the models we've tested and linked above. If a deal looks too good (a $300 espresso machine for $80), it's probably a different, worse model.

**5. Price check against camelcamelcamel**
Use camelcamelcamel.com to check a product's price history before buying. Some "deals" are just regular prices with a fake MSRP crossed out.
