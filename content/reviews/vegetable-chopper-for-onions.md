Onions are the most common chopping task in most kitchens — and the most dreaded. The tears, the uneven dice, the stray onion bits on the counter. A good vegetable chopper transforms onion prep from a tearful chore into a 10-second press.

## Why Onions Deserve Their Own Chopper

Not all vegetable choppers handle onions well. The ideal onion chopper needs:

- **Large hopper** — to fit a whole onion without pre-cutting
- **Sharp blades** — clean cuts release fewer tear-inducing compounds
- **Stable base** — firm vegetables like onion require pressure without slipping
- **Easy cleaning** — onion layers stick to blades more than other vegetables

## 3 Tips for Tear-Free Onion Chopping

1. **Chill the onion** — 15 minutes in the fridge reduces the volatile compounds that cause tears. A sharp blade also helps since it crushes fewer cell walls.

2. **Cut the root last** — The root end has the highest concentration of tear-inducing enzymes. Remove it after dicing, not before.

3. **Work fast** — The less time the cut onion sits exposed, the less time those compounds have to reach your eyes. A vegetable chopper completes the job in seconds — that alone reduces tears significantly.
