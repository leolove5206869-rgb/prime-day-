Oat milk froths well — but only barista blends. Our testing showed Oatly Barista and Califia Farms Barista produce dense microfoam nearly identical to whole milk. Standard oat milk (Oatly Original, Chobani Plain, generic store brands) produces watery, thin foam that collapses in under a minute.

## Tips for Frothing Oat Milk

**1. Use barista blends only.** Standard oat milk lacks the stabilizers (usually sunflower oil and dipotassium phosphate) that create stable foam. Barista blends are formulated specifically to froth.

**2. Don't overheat.** Oat milk proteins denature above 150°F. The ideal frothing temperature is 135-145°F. Automatic frothers handle this automatically. If using a handheld, microwave to 140°F then froth.

**3. Cold froth is easier.** Oat milk cold foam is more forgiving than hot. Use automatic frothers on cold setting or handheld on low speed with cold oat milk. Cold oat milk foam is excellent on iced lattes and cold brew.

**4. Shake the carton.** Oat milk separates in the carton. Always shake vigorously before pouring. This distributes stabilizers evenly and improves froth quality significantly.

**5. Clean immediately.** Oat milk residue dries harder than dairy. Rinse frother parts within 30 seconds of use or the residue bakes on.
